Privacy

hovering.today and the hovering.today connector · draft, last updated 2026-10-06

What we handle

  • Your music library — the titles, artists, albums, durations and links of songs in your likes and playlists on the platforms you connect.
  • Files you import — for Spotify, the “Account data” export you choose to upload. Only YourLibrary.json and Playlist*.json are read; everything else in the zip is ignored.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Your library is stored in your browser (IndexedDB on hovering.today) and matched there. hovering.today has no accounts and no server-side storage, and sends no analytics.

The connector extension

  • Only hovering.today can talk to it.
  • It asks for access to a platform (for example music.youtube.com) only when you click Connect, and only that platform.
  • It reads your library using the session you are already signed in with, by making requests from a tab on that platform’s own site. It never sees or stores your password, and it never sends your session anywhere else.
  • You can remove its access at any time in Chrome’s extension settings.

Removing your data

Use “Remove” next to a platform, or clear site data for hovering.today in your browser.
