Privacy

hovering.today · last updated 2026-10-07

hovering.today shows your likes and playlists from YouTube Music, Spotify and SoundCloud in one place. This page explains what it reads, where that goes, and how to remove it.

What we handle

  • Your music library: the titles, artists, durations and links of songs in your likes and playlists on the platforms you connect, and the names of those playlists.
  • Files you import: for Spotify, the “Account data” export you choose to upload. Only YourLibrary.json and Playlist*.json are read; everything else in the zip is ignored.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Your library is read and matched in your browser and stored there (IndexedDB on hovering.today). hovering.today has no accounts, no server-side storage of your data, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. We don’t sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

YouTube Music and Google sign-in

  • hovering.today uses YouTube API Services. By signing in you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google’s handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • When you click Sign in with Google, you grant read-only access (youtube.readonly). hovering.today uses it only to read your liked music and your playlists. It cannot post, like, delete or change anything, and it never sees your password.
  • The access token Google issues is held in your browser’s memory only for the sync and expires within an hour. It is never stored or sent anywhere except to Google’s YouTube API.
  • Liked videos that aren’t music are skipped and never stored.
  • You can remove hovering.today’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • hovering.today’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Use “Remove” next to a platform on hovering.today, or clear site data for hovering.today in your browser. Since nothing is stored on our side, that deletes everything.
